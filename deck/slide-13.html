CONCLUSION
What we learned
01
ViT transfers.
Pretrained ViT-B/32 reaches 97.57% on CIFAR-10 — 1.22 pp below the 98.79% reference, explained by a smaller budget.
02
The LR is the lever.
One hyperparameter — the paper's LR 0.003 — is worth 1.94 pp over the scaled-down alternative at the same seed.
03
Faster is nearly free.
PatchDropout cuts training time 15.7% for −0.10 pp — a practical speed-up for constrained hardware.
