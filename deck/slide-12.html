DISCUSSION
Honest limits
● Budget, not method
224 px · 1,000 steps vs the paper's 384 px · 10,000 steps — the 1.22 pp gap is fully explained by a smaller budget.
● One seed
Every result is a single run per condition; variance across seeds is not measured.
● Exploratory LR choice
The LR 0.003 run was chosen after earlier test scores were inspected — the cross-run comparison is exploratory, not confirmatory.
● Descriptive analysis
Attention and position-encoding findings describe this model; they do not prove a causal mechanism.
● Extension, not invention
PatchDropout is an existing method (Liu et al., 2022), applied through timm — a reproduction plus efficiency probe.
